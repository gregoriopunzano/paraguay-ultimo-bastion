# Paraguay, the Last Bastion / Paraguay, el último bastión

**Working Paper No. 1/2026 · Version 1.8 · 6 November 2026**
Gregorio Punzano · gregorio@satolink.com

A voluntary, verifiable Bitcoin adoption framework for Paraguay, drawn from the experiences of El Salvador, Bhutan and Estonia. The guaraní remains the sole legal tender.

Un marco de adopción voluntaria y verificable de Bitcoin para Paraguay, a partir de la experiencia de El Salvador, Bután y Estonia. El guaraní se mantiene como única moneda de curso legal.

## Key findings / Hallazgos principales

- Original price survey of the Spain–Paraguay corridor (61% of remittances): standard online costs of 3.5%–7.9%.
- Estimated total cost over Lightning with conversion into guaraníes: 2.4%–3.2% at today's retail rates; 1.7% with a licensed local partner.
- Household savings of up to USD 50.4 million per year; additional energy value of USD 11–157 million per year (scenarios).
- New metric: the Public Verifiability Index (PVI).
- Legal analysis: Phases 0–2 require no new legislation.
- Proportionality review of DNIT General Resolution 47/2026 on crypto-asset reporting, with a five-point reform proposal.
